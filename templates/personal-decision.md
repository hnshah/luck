# Personal Decision Context Template

## Decision
What choice are you making?

## Options
List the options.

## Desired Future State
What do you want this decision to make possible?

## Current Constraint
What is most limiting you right now?

## Active Demand Or Pull
Where is there real demand, energy, opportunity, or repeated pull?

## Adoption Friction
What would be hard to start, sustain, or explain?

## Maintenance Burden
What does each option require every week or month?

## Compounding Loop
What option creates learning, reputation, relationships, assets, or optionality?

## Circulation
Where does value flow back to you and the people around you?

## Integration
Which option connects more of your skills, network, and goals?

## Timing
What has to be true now for this to work?

## Evidence That Would Change The Decision
What new information would change your recommendation?
