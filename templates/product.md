# Product Context Template

## Goal
What outcome should the product create?

## User
Who has the problem?

## Active Demand
What urgent, recurring, expensive, or emotionally sharp pain exists?

## Current Alternative
What do users do today?

## Adoption Path
How does a user discover, try, adopt, and keep using this?

## Adoption Friction
What setup, trust, workflow, pricing, switching, or education barrier exists?

## Maintenance Burden
What must the team keep doing for the product to remain useful?

## Compounding Loop
Does usage create data, trust, content, distribution, network effects, or infrastructure?

## Circulation
Does value return to users, partners, builders, and the product?

## Integration
What systems, behaviors, tools, or markets must it connect with?

## Timing
Why is this possible now?

## Known Risk
What could make this temporary or fragile?
