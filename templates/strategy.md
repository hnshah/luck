# Strategy Context Template

## Decision
What choice needs to be made?

## Options
List the plausible options.

## Current Constraint
What is most limiting progress right now?

## Active Gradients
What demand, money, attention, trust, distribution, data, or capability is available?

## Resources
What time, people, capital, assets, or credibility can be used?

## Adoption Or Execution Path
What has to happen for the strategy to work?

## Maintenance Burden
What ongoing effort will the strategy require?

## Compounding Loop
What does success in step one make easier in step two?

## Circulation
Where does value flow back into the system?

## Integration
What existing ecosystem does this connect to or depend on?

## Timing
What prerequisites exist? What is missing? Is the window opening or closing?

## Evidence That Would Change The Decision
What signal would make you switch paths?
