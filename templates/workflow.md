# Workflow Context Template

## Goal
What output should the workflow reliably produce?

## Trigger
What starts the workflow?

## Inputs
What does it need to run?

## Output
What does done look like?

## Current Steps
List the main steps.

## Manual Rescue Points
Where does a human have to remember, inspect, patch, retry, or decide?

## Active Demand
Who needs this workflow and how often?

## Maintenance Burden
What breaks, drifts, or requires upkeep?

## Compounding Loop
What does each run produce that should improve future runs?

## Circulation
Where do learnings, fixes, templates, or QA rules flow back into the system?

## Integration
What tools, repos, dashboards, docs, or teams does this connect to?

## Known Failure
What is the repeated failure mode?
